ChatGPT Exporter reference
https://github.com/pionxzh/chatgpt-exporter

The Markdown export flow is adapted from the approach in src/api.ts
and src/exporter/markdown.ts: same-session conversation retrieval, traversal
of the current branch, filtering hidden/internal messages, and preserving
Markdown plus attachment names. The Zotero integration is implemented here.
The math delimiter convention also references src/utils/latex.ts,
with locally implemented code-span protection.
No Tampermonkey runtime or upstream application bundle is included.
